1878 — 2024

History

From a railway works team to champions of Europe. The red story, year by year.

Origins The Busby Years Wilderness The Ferguson Era Modern Era
Origins
1878

Newton Heath is born

Workers at the Lancashire and Yorkshire Railway's carriage works form Newton Heath LYR Football Club.

1892

Into the Football League

Newton Heath join the First Division of the expanding Football League.

1902

Saved, and renamed

Close to bankruptcy, the club is rescued by local businessmen led by John Henry Davies and becomes Manchester United.

1908

First league title

United are champions of England for the first time. The first FA Cup follows in 1909.

1910

Old Trafford opens

The club moves to its new home. It has played there ever since.

1941

The ground is bombed

German bombs wreck Old Trafford. United share Maine Road with City until they return home in 1949.

The Busby Years
1945

Matt Busby arrives

A former City player takes charge and begins to build a club around young talent.

1948

FA Cup winners

Busby wins his first trophy. The league title follows in 1952.

1956

The Busby Babes

A team of homegrown youngsters wins back-to-back titles in 1956 and 1957.

1958

Munich

On 6 February the team's plane crashes on take-off in Munich. Twenty-three people die, eight of them players. Busby survives, and so does a young Bobby Charlton.

1965

Champions again

Busby rebuilds around Charlton, Denis Law and George Best, and wins the title in 1965 and 1967.

1968

Kings of Europe

Ten years after Munich, United beat Benfica 4–1 at Wembley. They become the first English club to win the European Cup.

Wilderness
1974

Relegation

Six years after Wembley, United drop into the Second Division. They come straight back up in 1975.

1977

Cup days

FA Cup wins in 1977, 1983 and 1985 keep the fire alive, but the league title stays out of reach.

1986

Enter Ferguson

In November, Alex Ferguson arrives from Aberdeen.

The Ferguson Era
1990

The first trophy

An FA Cup win after a replay against Crystal Palace. The 1991 Cup Winners' Cup, won against Barcelona, follows.

1993

Twenty-six years over

United win the first Premier League title and end a 26-year wait to be champions.

1999

The Treble

League, FA Cup and Champions League. In Barcelona, Sheringham and Solskjær score in stoppage time to beat Bayern Munich 2–1.

2008

Moscow

Fifty years after Munich, United beat Chelsea on penalties in the rain to win a third European Cup.

2011

Nineteen

A 19th league title moves United past Liverpool's record of 18.

2013

Twenty, and farewell

Ferguson wins his 13th league title, the club's 20th, and retires after 26 years.

Modern Era
2016

FA Cup

A 2–1 win over Crystal Palace after extra time. It is the club's 12th FA Cup.

2017

Europa League

United beat Ajax in Stockholm to win the one major European trophy still missing from the cabinet.

2024

The city is red

United beat Manchester City 2–1 at Wembley to win a 13th FA Cup.

The story isn't finished.
Manchester is still Red.
